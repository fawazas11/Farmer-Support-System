Our Features

Connecting Farmers with Smart, Secure & Government-Backed Solutions

Our platform empowers farmers with modern digital tools, government services, and AI-based support. From disease detection to crop insurance and subsidies, everything is available in one place to improve productivity, reduce risk, and ensure stable income.

AI Crop Disease Prediction

Farmers can upload crop leaf images to detect diseases early using AI. The system provides disease name, severity, causes, and treatment recommendations to reduce crop loss and improve yield.

Farming Machinery Access

Farmers can view and purchase tractors, harvesters, sprayers, and other agricultural machinery listed through Krishi Bhavan or authorized sellers, with subsidy and pricing details.

Seeds & Young Crops

Purchase certified seeds and young plants directly from Krishi Bhavan and government-approved nurseries, ensuring healthy crops, transparent pricing, and disease-free planting material.

Crop Insurance Support

Farmers can apply for crop insurance schemes provided by the government. The platform allows uploading documents, tracking application status, and receiving claim updates to protect crops from natural disasters and losses.
